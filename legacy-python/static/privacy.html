Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy is important to us. ZCodeDev Social Publisher operates locally on your machine.

1. Data Collection & Storage

All API tokens, client secrets, and video files remain stored on your local device. We do not transmit or sell your credentials to any third-party servers.

2. Social Media Data

Access tokens are used solely to communicate directly with official platform APIs (Google YouTube API, Meta Graph API, TikTok API) for video uploads and publishing metadata.
